Key Advantages

  • Lightest: At just 1.45g, Remora 1 is the lightest controller tested
  • Smallest footprint: 380mm² PCB area - most compact design
  • Fastest starting: 6ms initial delay and 30ms to proper rotation
  • Lowest operating voltage: Starts reliably at just 1.35V
  • Best efficiency: Lower no-load current and superior MOSFET resistance (1.2mΩ)
  • Most controlled start: 14A peak current vs 20A+ for competitors

Note: All testing performed independently with identical 3000Kv motor and flywheel configuration. Results demonstrate consistent superior performance across multiple metrics. Download the complete pdf document.
