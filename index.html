Our Products

We design and manufacture speed controller boards (eComs) for slot car brushless motors. All the information for the Remora™ 1 is available on our wiki.

Speed Controller Boards

Electronic commutators (eComs) for brushless motors in slot cars. Fast, robust, and flexible.

  • Remora™ 1 - Double-sided board
  • Remora™ 2 - Single-sided board
  • Remora™ g - Genesis board (Sold exclusively by MidAmerica as Trixie 2.0)
  • Works with 2,000Kv to 22,500Kv+ motors

WiFi Link Programmer

Essential tool for programming and configuring your Remora™ boards.

  • Programs Remora™ 1 and Remora™ 2
  • Set all eCom parameters wirelessly
  • Complete kit or individual parts
  • One programmer serves all boards

WiFi Clamp

An important accessory for changing board settings with a motor attached.

  • Protects the WiFi link
  • Absorbs power from the motor

22,000Kv Motor

High-performance brushless motors for competitive slot car racing.

  • 1105 size, 6 magnet design
  • Proven in eurosport racing
  • Runs perfectly with Remora™ 1
  • 13.68mm diameter, 1.5mm shaft

22,000Kv Motor

Limited supply of highly competitive brushless motors. Used successfully by well-known racers in eurosport and unlimited club racing events.

Installation Note: Some motor mounts need improved clearance at the front of the motor. Do this by either grinding a very small amount of metal off the edge of the circlip or by removing it entirely and adding spacers to the motor shaft before attaching a pinion. Ask if you need help ...
